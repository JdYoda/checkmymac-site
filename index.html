What the app checks

SystemWhat's tested
MAC PASSPORTModel, serial number, country of sale and manufacture, build date. A "Check Apple warranty" button, plus flags when the Mac has Activation Lock or mobile device management on.
BATTERYCycle count, real capacity and battery condition. Charging is checked separately, over USB-C and MagSafe.
PORTSPorts are checked one by one. Plug in a cable or a drive and the matching port lights up on the Mac diagram. If a port doesn't respond, or drops when you wiggle the plug, you see it right away.
SENSORSTemperatures, fans, ambient light sensor, lid angle, sleep sensor. Readings come straight from the hardware, and anything off is highlighted.
TOUCH IDThe app counts touches on the sensor. It needs no enrolled fingerprints, so the test works on a wiped Mac too.
SSDDrive SMART: wear, reallocated blocks, terabytes written. Then a speed test: the app writes a 5 GB file and reads it back, so the cache doesn't skew the result.
AUDIOLeft and right speaker separately, a frequency sweep and music. The app shows where sound goes right now: speakers, headphones or an external device. The microphone is checked with a recording up to a minute, played back.
STRESSThe app loads the CPU or GPU for a set time and draws one graph with two lines: performance and temperature. If performance drops under load, the graph shows it.
REPORTA PDF with a verdict on every item. In the Service version: work order number, client name, and a comparison of the before and after-repair checks.

How a check works

  1. Open the app. No installation. It detects the Mac model and builds the checklist for it.
  2. Go through the tests top to bottom. The app runs some on its own; for the rest it shows what to press and where to look: screen, sound, keyboard, sensors.
  3. Save the report. Press "Finish test / Report". In the PDF, each item shows OK or the name of the defect.

Is it safe to run on a Mac I don't own yet?

Yes. The app is notarized by Apple, changes nothing on the machine, writes no fingerprints, installs no drivers and needs no internet. It reads hardware state and shows it to you.

Is the download safe?

The DMG is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and it's served from GitHub Releases over HTTPS. Gatekeeper verifies the signature the first time you open it.

Which Macs are supported?

MacBook, MacBook Air and MacBook Pro on macOS 10.15 Catalina or newer — both Intel and Apple Silicon. The checklist adapts automatically to the exact model, using a built-in database of 70+ MacBook models.

Do I need the internet?

The free version never needs it. A paid license goes online once, to activate — after that diagnostics run fully offline, including on Macs that have never been connected.

How do updates work?

The app checks a signed update feed and installs new versions in one click. Updates are free on every tier.

What languages does it speak?

English and Russian — the interface, the test instructions and the PDF reports.

What if it doesn't work on my Mac?

Try the free version first — it never expires and shows how the app runs on your exact Mac. If you buy the full version and it fails to work on your Mac, email us within 14 days and tell us what went wrong: we fix it, or refund the purchase and deactivate the license.

Who makes this?

The app was built inside a large Apple repair service: it grew out of the checklist the bench runs on every incoming MacBook. It is published and maintained by an independent developer.
